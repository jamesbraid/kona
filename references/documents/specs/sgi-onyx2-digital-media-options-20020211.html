Digital Media

Among the most demanding of computational requirements are the input, processing and distribution of the various digital media types. To address these requirements SGI offers a family of input, output, compression, and advanced display options for the Onyx2 product line. By taking advantage of the scalability, I/O bandwidth, and graphics power of Onyx2, this new family of options allows users to configure systems with digital media capabilities that far exceed those of any other computer systems on the market today.

Graphics-to-Video Option (GVO)

For applications requiring CCIR 601 digital video and alpha output from graphics in real time, GVO provides a low-latency solution.

Digital Video Option (DIVO)

DIVO routes multiple video and audio streams to and from system memory or disk. In conjunction with the Onyx2 graphics subsystem, broadcast video maps directly onto any three-dimensional surface while image processing functions enhance the video stream in real-time.

DIVO connects Onyx2 systems to industry-standard CCIR 601, SMPTE 259, and SMPTE 272 equipment.

GVO and DIVO together are ideal for virtual sets where video is input to graphics and output in real time, and for content creation applications for simultaneous content input and output.

Professional Audio Sampling and Playback

All Onyx2 systems include 24-bit digital audio input and output. The Onyx2 audio system provides 3.5mm and RCA jack connectors for connection to analog audio equipment, such as the included amplified speakers. Optical connectors and 75 ohm BNC jacks connect Onyx2 with AES/EBU and ADAT 8-channel equipment for up to 10 channels of 24-bit digital audio. Built-in video reference input and loop-out lock Onyx2 audio to a video house sync signal.
